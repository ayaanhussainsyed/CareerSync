# 🌐 CareerSync
Find your future — intelligently.
CareerSync helps users discover, visualize, and prepare for their ideal careers through AI-powered self-discovery tools. Using intelligent agents, machine learning, and an intuitive interface, CareerSync makes career guidance personal, predictive, and practical.

# 🚀 Features
🧬 DNA Mapping
Answer a few interactive questions and let our ML model decode your professional “DNA.”
Multiple specialized agents analyze your responses from different perspectives, and a base model synthesizes their insights into one cohesive career summary — uniquely tailored to you.
🔮 Future You
Ever wondered what your day-to-day life would look like in your dream career?
“Future You” simulates a realistic workday in that role — so you can experience your future before you choose it.
📚 GradeScope
For high school students deciding on academic streams, GradeScope combines your grades, interests, and goals to suggest the most aligned academic and career paths.
🏢 Leadership Lab
Step into a virtual office and practice leadership like never before.
Talk to fine-tuned AI agents who simulate real workplace dynamics — helping you grow your communication, decision-making, and management skills in a lifelike environment.


# 🧠 Tech Stack
Layer	Technology
Frontend / Web App	Flask
AI / Orchestration	LangChain
Database	MongoDB
ML & Agent Logic	Custom-trained models + chained agents for analysis and simulation
🧩 How It Works
User Input: Interactive Q&A or grade-based data.
Analysis: Multiple domain-specific agents evaluate user input.
Synthesis: A base model combines all results into a unified profile.
Simulation: Optional modules like Future You or Leadership Lab bring insights to life.
🌱 Vision
CareerSync isn’t just about career guidance — it’s about building a mirror to your future self.
We blend psychology, data, and simulation to help users see and feel what success truly means for them.
